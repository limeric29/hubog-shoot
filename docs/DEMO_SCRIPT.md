# Hubog Shoot — 3-Minute Demo Script

Target: Meta AI Developer Hackathon submission video (≤ 3:00) + X post.
Tone: warm, seller-first, English with a light Filipino touch. Show the app, not slides.

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem

> "If you sell calamansi juice, or candles, or anything handmade on Shopee and TikTok —
> you know photos decide whether people buy. But a real product shoot costs money,
> takes a whole day, and still gives you one look. Most sellers just post one dim
> photo and hope. That's the gap Hubog Shoot closes."

On screen: a dull phone photo of a product vs. a finished 5-photo pack, side by side.

## 0:25–1:10 — The shoot (live)

> "I take one photo of my product — this homemade calamansi juice — add one line:
> 'Homemade calamansi juice, 300ml, ₱120, no preservatives.' And press Make my photos."

On screen: upload, brief, verification, click. While it works:

> "Muse Spark reads the photo and my words, plans the shoot — main, lifestyle,
> detail, promo — then directs each scene. Every photo is checked against my
> original, so the label and bottle stay true. If a scene drifts, it reshoots —
> or places my real cutout on the set."

On screen: results land, 5 of 5. Scroll the pack.

## 1:10–1:50 — Listing + animation (the wow)

> "Captions, hashtags, and listing text come with the pack — copy straight into Shopee.
> And watch this: one click animates any scene. Muse Spark directs the motion,
> LTX 2.5 renders it on a B300."

On screen: copy listing text, click Animate, play the 5-second video full-screen.

## 1:50–2:30 — Honesty + craft

> "Two things matter to us. First, honesty: AI scenes are labeled AI, and the main
> photo is always your real product. Second, it's free to try — three shoots and
> three animations, then a free account for a daily quota."

On screen: quota line, "AI-generated scene" badge, sign-in modal briefly.

## 2:30–3:00 — Close

> "One photo in, a whole listing out. Hubog Shoot — product photos for your shop.
> Try it live at shoot.hubog.app."

On screen: the pack ZIP downloading, URL + QR on the end card.

---

## Research points (for submission answers / pinned comment)

- Listings with multiple quality images convert far better than single-photo ones;
  marketplaces rank complete listings higher in search and recommendations.
- Short product video (5–15s) lifts engagement on TikTok Shop / Reels-style
  surfaces; sellers who post video report higher add-to-cart rates.
- Small sellers cite photography cost and time as the top blocker to listing
  quality — a phone-first, minutes-long flow removes both.
- Trust angle: visible "AI scene vs real product" labeling matches incoming
  marketplace disclosure expectations and keeps buyers confident.
